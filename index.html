About

A short introduction

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Tehran, specializing in Artificial Intelligence, and I work as an AI engineer. I enjoy finding patterns in human and machine behavior and turning them into systems that are both useful and interpretable. A common thread across my research and applied work is explainable AI: building models whose decisions people can actually understand and trust.

For the past two years I have been a researcher at the Human and Robot Interaction Lab (TaarLab), directed by Prof. M. Tale Masouleh. Within our group's work on robotic scene rearrangement, my focus is on how interaction with an environment can become a step toward interpreting and effectively reorganizing it.

I also worked with the University of Tehran's team for the SHL 2025 Challenge, supervised by Prof. B. Babaali, where we explored time-series foundation models for Human Activity Recognition. That work earned 1st place and a paper at UbiComp 2025. As an AI Engineer at KareOne, I work more directly with large language models and data: I built the company's retrieval-augmented (RAG) assistant for querying large document collections, along with data-analysis tools that turn numeric and textual data into clear, explainable business insights.
